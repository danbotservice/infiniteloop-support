InfiniteLoop — Support

InfiniteLoop lets you direct long-running coding agents (Claude Code, Codex) on your own Mac or Linux computer from your iPhone or iPad, over SSH.

Getting started

  • Choose Explore a demo when that option is shown to see sample sessions in Mission Control without connecting a computer or entering SSH credentials.
  • To connect your own machine: enter its name or IP address (Tailscale names work too), your username, and the sign-in method requested by the app. When password sign-in is used, the password is stored in your device's Keychain and may be used to install and use the device's SSH key. The connection endpoint and session settings are stored locally in the app.

Requirements

A Mac or Linux computer you can reach (on the same network or via Tailscale) with SSH enabled and tmux installed.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No.
  • Where are my connection details stored? The endpoint and session settings are stored locally on your device. Passwords, SSH identity keys, and host-trust records use the device Keychain. They are used to connect directly to the computer you selected.
  • What happens when I tap a terminal link? Supported HTTP and HTTPS links open in your browser. The website and browser handle that page; InfiniteLoop does not receive the browser page contents.
  • Which agents are supported? Claude Code and Codex, running in your terminal.

Contact & help
