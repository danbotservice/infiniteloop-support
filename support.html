InfiniteLoop — Support

InfiniteLoop lets you direct long-running coding agents (Claude Code, Codex) on your own Mac or Linux computer from your iPhone or iPad, over SSH.

Getting started

  • Tap Explore a demo on the connect screen to see how it works with no setup.
  • To connect your own machine: enter its name or IP address (Tailscale names work too), your username, and your login password once. InfiniteLoop then sets up its own key so you won't need the password again. Nothing is stored outside your device.

Requirements

A Mac or Linux computer you can reach (on the same network or via Tailscale) with SSH enabled and tmux installed.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No.
  • Where are my credentials stored? Only in your device's Keychain. They never leave your device except to connect to your machine.
  • Which agents are supported? Claude Code and Codex, running in your terminal.

Contact & help

Please open an issue and we'll help. We aim to respond within a few business days.
