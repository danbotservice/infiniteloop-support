Infinite-Loop

Your own computers, on your iPhone and iPad.

Infinite-Loop connects straight to your own Mac or Linux computers over SSH. It puts tmux terminals, your files and your Mac's desktop in one app, side by side on iPad.

Coming soon to the App Store · Free · iPhone and iPad, iOS 26 or later

What it does

  • Real tmux sessions. Start something long, put the phone away, come back. It kept running on your computer.
  • Every session on every computer in one list.
  • Files. Read and edit files on your computer, and see what changed in a project.
  • Your Mac's desktop in a pane, using macOS Screen Sharing inside the same SSH connection. Nothing to install on the Mac.
  • Links open beside the terminal. Sign-in links that a program prints open in a browser pane.
  • A demo you can try with no computer and no network.

What it doesn't do

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • No relay and no server of ours. Your device talks directly to your computer.
  • No analytics and no tracking. Passwords and keys stay in this device's Keychain and are never synced.
  • It doesn't run AI agents itself. Anything that runs in your terminal works, including coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex, under your own accounts with them.

What you need

A Mac or Linux computer you can reach, with SSH turned on (on a Mac: Remote Login) and tmux installed. For the Mac desktop: Screen Sharing turned on. Tailscale works but isn't required. Setup help.
