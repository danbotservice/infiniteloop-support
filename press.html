Infinite-Loop — Press kit

Fact sheet

  • Name: Infinite-Loop
  • Made by: Dan Fishkin, independent developer
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad, iOS and iPadOS 26 or later
  • Price: Free. No in-app purchases, no ads, no account
  • Released: coming soon to the App Store

In one paragraph

Infinite-Loop is a native iPhone and iPad app that puts your own Mac and Linux computers in one place: tmux terminals, files and the Mac's desktop, over a direct SSH connection with no account and no relay. It's built for people who run long jobs, such as coding agents, on their own computers and want to check in from wherever they are.

What's in it

  • Real tmux sessions that keep running with the phone away
  • Every session on every computer in one list
  • Files: read, edit, and see what changed in a project
  • The Mac's desktop in a pane through macOS Screen Sharing inside the SSH connection, with nothing to install on the Mac
  • A browser pane for links printed in the terminal
  • A demo that needs no computer and no network
