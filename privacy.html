InfiniteLoop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

InfiniteLoop is a tool for directing your own coding agents on your own computers. It is designed to keep your data on your device and under your control.

Data handling

InfiniteLoop operates no analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry service. We do not operate a service that receives terminal, file, session, or attachment content.

Your connection details stay on your device

When you connect InfiniteLoop to your own Mac or Linux computer, the endpoint and session settings you enter are stored locally in the app on your device. Passwords, SSH identity keys, and host-trust records are stored in the device Keychain. They are used to establish the SSH connection to the computer you chose and are not sent to InfiniteLoop.

Your sessions are yours

Terminal output, files, and any content from the computers you connect to are relayed directly between your device and your machine over an encrypted SSH connection. We do not see, store, or process that content.

Photos you attach

If you attach a photo to send to your agent, it is uploaded directly to the computer you are connected to, for that agent to use. It is not sent to us.

No account

InfiniteLoop requires no account or sign-in with us.

Links and websites

When you activate a supported HTTP or HTTPS link from a terminal, InfiniteLoop opens it in your browser. InfiniteLoop does not receive or store the browser page contents; the website and browser handle that activity under their own terms and policies.

Children

InfiniteLoop is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue on our support page.
